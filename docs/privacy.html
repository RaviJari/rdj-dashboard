Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

RDJ Dashboard ("the extension") is a new-tab dashboard for Chrome. It is designed to be private by default: there is no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no servers operated by the developer. All data you create lives in your own Chrome profile and is synced only through your own Google account.

Data we store

Everything is stored locally on your device. The extension may store:

  • Your dashboard settings, theme, and widget layout
  • Your active profile (Personal/Work) and per-profile quick links, pinned bookmarks, and focus entries
  • Your todos and notes

When Chrome's "Sync" is enabled and your Chrome extensions sync is turned on, these items are mirrored to Chrome sync storage, which is synchronized across your devices through your Google account by Google's own infrastructure. We never see this data.

Bookmarks

With your permission, the extension reads your Chrome bookmarks (in Chrome, extension "bookmarks" permission) so it can display and search them. Bookmarks are never transmitted anywhere; they are processed entirely on your device.

Third-party services

The extension calls a few public, keyless APIs only for live data:

  • Open-Meteo / Nominatim / IP-API — to fetch weather and approximate location. Your IP address or exact location is never stored by the extension; these calls happen directly from your browser.
  • Unsplash CDN — to load decorative background photos.
  • Google & DuckDuckGo — to fetch website favicons.

None of these providers are given your personal data beyond standard request metadata (e.g. an IP address), as with any website you visit.

Deletion

Uninstalling the extension removes orphaned local data in Chrome. To stop syncing, turn off Chrome extensions sync or sign out of your Google account. You can delete your synced data by opening chrome://settings/syncSetup → Your Google Account → Data & privacy → Your data in Chrome and Google Account.
