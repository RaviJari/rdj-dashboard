Your command center.
Every new tab.

RDJ Dashboard turns Chrome's new tab into a Momentum-style home — a giant clock, your focus, weather, pins and todos — with a Spotlight-style ⌘K search and Google-account sync across devices.

Install from the latest release View source on GitHub

⌘K floating search

Spotlight-style overlay that fuzzy-searches your bookmarks with favicons and keyboard navigation.

Personal & Work profiles

Independent quick links, pinned bookmarks and focus for both of your lives.

Google-account sync

Settings, links, pins, focus, todos and notes follow you across devices.

Weather

Live conditions via Open-Meteo with auto-detect or a chosen city, cached wisely.

Widgets

Draggable, resizable, hideable tiles — clock, notes, todos, pins and more.

Popup + new tab

One-click toolbar popup with your pins, quick links and todos; links open in new tabs.
